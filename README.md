

Prodify is a smart dashboard that makes your workday awesome. It shows you if you're super focused (that's "FlowState") and warns you if you're getting overwhelmed (the S.O.S. score). The app actually figures out your most important tasks for the day and tells you the best time to do them, tailored just for you. Plus, it gives you ideas to boost your focus and even reminds you about good work habits, like quickly jotting down tiny tasks or taking a short break.

To get started, take a look at src/app/page.tsx.
