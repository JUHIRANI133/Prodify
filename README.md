

Prodify is a smart dashboard that makes your workday awesome. It shows you if you're productivity score (that's "Prodify Score") and helps you try different productivity enhancingtechnique whichever is best suited for you (the favourability score). The app actually figures out your most important tasks for the day and tells you the best time to do them, tailored just for you. Plus, it gives you ideas to boost your focus through a chatbot and even reminds you about good work habits and recommends a book you can read, like quickly jotting down tiny tasks or taking a short break.
